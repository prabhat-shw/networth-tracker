# M5 — Dashboard, goals & insights

**Goal:** the household sees its number move over time, sees what every rupee is *for*, and
gets honest answers to "am I on track" and "why did it move". Everything is computed on the
device from data that is already decrypted. M5 adds no new server surface except one public
price series (#30).

## What exists after M4

- Every holding is valued on read (`valuedItems`). `allLenses()` gives per-lens totals, and
  `earmarks()` gives per-goal claims and Unallocated. The lens choice lives on Home only.
- One `snapshot` record per household per day (ADR-0070), with every lens, per-bucket
  composition and `pricesAsOf`. Nothing draws them yet.
- Goals: CRUD, earmarked totals, the Unallocated card. No status, progress, SIP or detail
  screen. Tagging happens only on the account form ("Tag to goal").
- `transaction` records and `contributions()` exist, but no UI writes a transaction. Tax lots
  and `consumeFifo` exist with no sell flow.
- Recharts is a dependency, but no chart ships yet.

## Design questions the first issue settles (ADR-0071)

1. **Lens store.** Recommended: one Zustand store per device (not synced, not persisted
   across lock). Home, Accounts, Goals and the timeline all read it. This closes ADR-0059's
   deferral.
2. **Timeline series.** One point per snapshot date plus today's live figure. Gaps are not
   interpolated: the line joins real points, and the table lists only real dates. Former
   members come from `formerMembers`. Recommended: the series is a pure function of
   snapshots, in `src/domain/timeline.ts`.
3. **Goal maths and rounding.** A growth factor such as `(1+i)^years` is dimensionless.
   Recommended: compute factors in float64 from basis points, and round half-up to paise
   once per money result. Money itself never becomes a float, and the ADR states this
   boundary. Months run from today to the target date, using an effective monthly rate of
   `(1+r)^(1/12) − 1`. The SIP is an ordinary annuity, paid at month end. Status:
   **on track** when projected ≥ 100% of the inflated target, **at risk** at ≥ 80%,
   **off track** below that. A past or zero-month target is an error state, never NaN.
4. **Current SIP.** UX §3.6 shows "has ₹9,000/mo". Recommended: an optional
   `monthlyContribution` on the goal (paise, user-entered), added under the evolution codec.
   M6 can derive it from imported transactions later.
5. **Auto-tag rules.** Recommended: no new record type. A rule lives on the goal it feeds
   (`autoTag: { kinds[], instrumentCodes[], bps }`). Rules pre-fill tags on create or edit,
   and a bulk action the user confirms applies them to untagged records. A rule never
   rewrites an existing tag, and never rewrites a record without a user action.
6. **Transaction entry scope.** XIRR needs real cash-flow dates. Recommended: a minimal
   in/out leg editor and a linked transfer on an account. Lots and the sell flow
   (`consumeFifo`) stay in M6, where imports supply lots.
7. **XIRR inputs.** The opening value is the first snapshot inside the window, or the
   holding's value on its first transaction. Flows are the window's transactions, and the
   terminal value is today's valuation. Excluded per UX §11.2: property, gold, money lent.
   EPF/PPF/NPS/SSY show a declared rate instead.
8. **History for #29 and #30.** Universes go back only to M4's first ingest. Recommended:
   commission drag is computed "since we have prices" (the later of the lot date and the
   oldest stored NAV pair), and the copy says so. The Nifty 50 TRI becomes a new universe kind,
   with a one-off load of its public history file (a whole series, so no leak). Re-verify the
   source URL in #30.

## Acceptance

- The timeline draws every snapshot in every lens, and switching the lens anywhere holds
  everywhere.
- Each goal shows its inflated target, progress, required SIP and status. All four match
  hand-computed fixtures to the paisa, and a bad assumption shows an Alert, never NaN.
- Unallocated reaches zero through the editor, bulk tagging or a rule, and a second member
  sees the same allocations after sync.
- XIRR matches a spreadsheet fixture. Insights cards render, or show an honest "come back
  later" when their history is missing.
- No new request names a holding (the TRI is one public series). The DB scan still finds no
  plaintext.
